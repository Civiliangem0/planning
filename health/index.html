Health

Health per body part, the Tarkov way. A headshot kills, legs can be destroyed, and the ammo you load and the armour you wear decide everything in between. It starts with seven basic guns and five calibres.

Plan · nothing built yet Agreed 1 Oct 2026 Tarkov for health, ammo and armour

The body

Seven parts · 440 health
  • Head or thorax at 0 kills. Every round we have kills with one hit to a bare head. The neck counts as the thorax, as in Tarkov since 2025.
  • Arms, legs and the stomach can be destroyed. Later hits on a destroyed part spread evenly over the rest of the body: ×0.49 from an arm, ×0.7 from a leg, ×1.05 from the stomach. Shooting only the legs still kills, eventually.
  • Destroyed parts hurt you. A leg: half speed and no sprinting. An arm: slower reloads, draws and aiming. The stomach: hunger and thirst drain five times faster.
  • No more headshot ×2. The head's small health does that job. NPCs with a human body get the same seven parts.
Fatal at 0Can be destroyed

On the Tab screen each part shows its health in Tarkov's colours: green, then yellow, then red, and black when destroyed. A small body figure appears on the HUD for a few seconds when you are hurt or healed.

Armour

Tarkov's classes 1–6 · one set of rules

The class (1 to 6, for body armour and helmets alike) says how hard a piece is to get through. New, it resists about 10 × its class; worn, less. A round with about that much penetration or more gets through.

  • Class 3 stops pistols. Rifles get through.
  • Class 4 stops most normal rifle rounds. Armour-piercing rounds get through.
  • Class 5 lets only armour-piercing rifle rounds through.
  • Class 6 stops nearly everything we have. Classes 1 and 2 stop little.

Stopped

A stopped round still bruises. How much depends on the piece and how far the round fell short of getting through.

damage × bruise share × clamp(1 − 0.03 × (resistance − pen), 0.2, 1)

Through

A round that gets through keeps 60% to 100% of its damage, more the further its penetration beats the armour.

damage × clamp(pen ÷ (resistance + 12), 0.6, 1)

Each piece copies a Tarkov item

The rules never name a piece. Every vest, carrier and helmet copies the Tarkov item closest to it: its class, its durability (one plate's, about 40 to 60), its material (how fast it wears), its bruise share, what it covers and how much it slows you. So two pieces of the same class still behave differently, as in Tarkov.

Other damage

  • Falls hit the legs, as in Tarkov. What the legs can't take passes on.
  • Melee lands on the part it hits. Fists can't kill.
  • Explosions take the same share of every part they took of 100 before, so grenade distances stay the same.
  • Hunger and thirst at 0 wear every part down to 20% but never kill.

Recovering

  • The medkit works like Tarkov's car kit: head and thorax first, then the most hurt part, from 220 points kept on the item. No more overheal.
  • Healing by itself after a minute without damage, at Tarkov's out-of-raid rate: about an hour from nearly dead to full.
  • Destroyed parts come back only through a medic or the hospital, and later surgical kits.
  • Respawn at full health for now. Tarkov's 30% comes once the hospital heals.

What Tarkov players complain about

And what we do
  • "Head, eyes": one-shot heads end fights in a blink. Kept, because headshots should kill. NPCs must not aim for heads unfairly.
  • The leg meta: legs have no armour, so strong rounds kill armoured players through the legs. Kept at Tarkov's softened level and watched in testing.
  • Limbs that are just health bars: a legged enemy keeps running. Ours slow down on destroyed legs.
  • Painkillers all the time cancel every penalty. Watched when painkillers arrive.
  • Too many rounds to learn: Tarkov players live on outside charts. Ours: 13 types with the same three names, and inspect says what each does.
